## Expression
What time is it?/What's the time?
- 顺序（past; half; a quarter）
  分pass时(分钟<=30min): half pass 12./a quarter past 12.
- 直接说=> 12: 50 => It's twelve fifty.
- 倒序(to; half;  a quarter)
  分to时(分钟>30min), hour+1
  a quarter to 12. => 11:45
  ten to 12             => 11:50
quarter one => 12:45
