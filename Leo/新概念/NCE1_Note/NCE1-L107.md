# Grammar
## adj. ⽐较级&最⾼级
````col
```col-md
构成：1 
单⾳节形容词+er/est 
small → smaller → smallest  
new → newer → newest  
old → older → oldest
```
```col-md
构成：2 
单词以e结尾+r/st 
large → larger → largest  
nice → nicer → nicest
```
````

````col
```col-md
构成：3 
辅⾳字⺟+y结尾的，把y变为i, 加er, est  
pretty → prettier → prettiest 
easy → easier → easiest 
heavy → heavier → heaviest
```
```col-md
构成：4 
辅+元+辅，双写末尾辅⾳字⺟，加er, est  
big → bigger → biggest 
hot → hotter → hottest 
fat → fatter → fattest
```
````

````col
```col-md
adj. ⽐较级⽤法  
主语+be+adj.er+than+⽐较对象 
That man is older than this woman.  
Yao is taller than Jet Li.  
Jet Li is shorter than Yao.  
  
The left dog is smaller than the right dog.  
The right dog is bigger than the left dog.  
  
The left shoes are newer than the 
right shoes.  
```
````

````col
```col-md
adj. 最⾼级⽤法  
主语+be+the+adj.est+n.+范围  
  
主语+be+the+adj.est+n.+范围  
My father is the coolest guy in the world.  
My mom is the kindest person in the world.  
Angelina Jolie is the hottest woman in Hollywood.
```
```col-md
adj. 最⾼级⽤法  
主语+be+**the**+adj.est+n.+I’ve ever met/seen/know/done
This is the craziest thing I’ve ever done.  
This is the cutest baby I’ve ever seen.  
Charles Chaplin is the funniest guy I’ve ever seen.  
Mother Teresa is the kindest woman I’ve ever known.
```
````
