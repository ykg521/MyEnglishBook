# Words & Grammar & Essay
Lesson 41 Penny's bag 彭妮的提包
## Essay
SAM      : Is that bag heavy, Penny?
PENNY : Not very.
SAM      :  Here! Put it on this chair. What's in it?
PENNY : A piece of cheese.
PENNY : A loaf of bread.
PENNY : A bar of soap.
PENNY : A bar of chocolate.
PENNY : A bottle of milk.
PENNY : A pound of sugar.
PENNY : Half a pound of coffee.
PENNY : A quarter of a pound of tea.
PENNY : And a tin of tobacco.
SAM      : Is that tin of tobacco for me?
PENNY : Well, it's certainly not for me!
