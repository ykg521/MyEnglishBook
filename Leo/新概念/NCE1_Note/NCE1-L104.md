```` col
too + adj.  
too adj.+(for sb)+ to do
否定表达: 过于...不可以...
It’s too big for me to wear.
```
```col-md
adj. + enough  
adj.+enough+(for sb)+ to do
肯定表达: 足够...可以...
It’s big enough for me to wear.
```
````
