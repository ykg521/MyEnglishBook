# Grammar
现在进行时
含义：一个动作现在正在进行
现在进行时：肯
结构：主+am/is/are+doing (v.ing 现在分词)
1. v.+ing: eating/opening/reading
   She is reading a book.
2. 去e+ing(e不发音）
   come-coming/make-making/dance-dancing
3. 双写+ing
   run-running/sit-sitting/swim-swimming

现在进行时：否
1. am/is/are+not+doing
   She is not reading books

现在进行时：一般
1. am/is/are(提句首)+主+doing? 
   Is she opening the door?

现在进行时：特殊
1. 结构： What+am/is/are+主+doing?
   What are they doing?
   Are they dancing?
   Yes.

# Essay
JEAN : Where7 s Sally, Jack?
JACK : She's in the garden, Jean.
JEAN : What's she doing?
JACK : She's sitting under the tree.
JEAN : Is Tim in the garden, too?
JACK : Yes, he is.
He's climbing the tree.
JEAN : I beg your pardon?
Who's climbing the tree?
JACK : Tim is.
JEAN : What about the dog?
JACK : The dog's in the garden, too. It's running across the grass. It's running after a cat.
