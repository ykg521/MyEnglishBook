# Grammar
## 一般现在时含义：
- 客观事实/存在状态L51-54
- 习惯动作L55-58
I want this job.
I love Beijing.
I like tea.
He wants this job, too.
- does表强调：
  He does want this job.
  He loves Beijing, too.
  She likes tea, too.
  They want some money.
### v. s 第三人称单数
v. + s:
- likes; wants
  He likes tea.
s/x/ch/sh/o 结尾的v.加es:
- watches; goes; washes; does
  He washes dishes every day.
辅音字幕+y结尾，变y为i+es:
- cry-cries; fry-fries
  The baby cries every night.
元音字母+y结尾,直接加：
- buy-buys; say-says/sez/
  He buys a new phone every year.
一般现在时：否定(三单，does+not)
- He wants this job.
  He doesn't want this job.
- She like tea.
  She doesn't like tea.
- She washes dishes every day.
一般现在时：一般疑问(三单，does+not)

# Essay
BUTCHER:  Do you want any meat today, Mrs. Bird? 
MRS. BIRD: Yes, please. 
BUTCHER:  Do you want beef or lamb? 
MRS. BIRD: Beef, please. 
BUTCHER:  This lamb's very good. 
MRS. BIRD: I like lamb, but my husband doesn't. 
BUTCHER:  What about some steak? This is a nice piece. 
MRS. BIRD: Give me that piece, please. 
MRS. BIRD: And a pound of mince, too. 
BUTCHER:  Do you want a chicken, Mrs. Bird? They're very nice. 
MRS. BIRD: No, thank you. 
MRS. BIRD: My husband likes steak, but he doesn't like chicken. 
BUTCHER:  To tell you the truth, Mrs. Bird, I don't like chicken either!
