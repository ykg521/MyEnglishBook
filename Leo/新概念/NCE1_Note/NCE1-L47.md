# Words & Grammar & Essay
Lesson 47 A cup of coffee 一杯咖啡
## Essay
CHRISTINE: Do you like coffee, Ann? 
ANN:              Yes, I do. 
CHRISTINE: Do you want a cup? 
ANN:              Yes, please, Christine. 
CHRISTINE: Do you want any sugar? 
ANN:              Yes, please. 
CHRISTINE: Do you want any milk? 
ANN:              No, thank you. I don't like milk in my coffee. I like black coffee. 
CHRISTINE: Do you like biscuits? 
ANN:              Yes, I do. 
CHRISTINE: Do you want one? 
ANN:              Yes, please.

## Grammer
### 一般现在时含义：
- 客观事实/存在状态L51-54
- 习惯动作L55-58
#### 肯：
- I want this job./I love Beijing./I like tea./I do like tea.
#### 否：do not = don't
- I don't like this job./I don't like tea./
一般疑问：Do 提到句首
- Do you want this job.
  Yes, I Do./No, I don't.
- Do you love me?
  Yes, I do./No, I don't.
#### 特殊疑问句：特殊疑问词+一般疑问句(去掉答案)
- I want this job.
  I do want this job.
  Do you want this job?
  Why do you want this job?
  What do you want?
